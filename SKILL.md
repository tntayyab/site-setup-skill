## ⛔ Strict Zero-Tolerance Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1. **NO DUAL SEARCH BARS:**
   - Under no circumstances may a page have two search bars.
   - If the header has a search icon/bar, disable any hero search bar or duplicate homepage search boxes.

2. **PERFECT 3-COLUMN LEVELED FOOTER:**
   - Column 1: Brand About / Mission bio (2-3 concise editorial sentences).
   - Column 2: Quick Links / Categories Menu.
   - Column 3: Legal & Compliance Pages (About Us, Contact Us, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, Disclaimer, Affiliate Disclosure).
   - Both column headers and list items must align symmetrically with balanced spacing.

3. **100% TEXTLESS CATEGORY & HERO IMAGES:**
   - All category cards ("Browse by topic") and Hero banner slides must use **100% photographic, textless, WebP images**.
   - Absolutely NO text overlays, typography, blog post titles, or badges embedded inside the background image.

4. **THEMIXIFY MANDATORY CORE SETTINGS:**
   - **General:** Site Width = `1400px`.
   - **Trending Bars:** Sticky trending posts bar = ON, Top bar popular posts = ON, Posts bar auto scroll = ON.
   - **Bottom Bars:** Bottom bar latest posts = OFF, Show featured image on single posts = OFF.
   - **Share Bar:** "Share row under the title" = OFF (disabled).
   - **Media Resizer:** All toggles = ON (Images: 600x400 px centered, Videos: 600x338 px 16:9 centered).
   - **404 Redirect:** "Send all 404s to homepage" = ON.

5. **UNIQUE EEAT AUTHOR PROFILES:**
   - Never reuse stock images across different sites for different authors.
   - Each author must have a unique, niche-appropriate WebP persona avatar, verified bio, and social links (GitHub, LinkedIn/FB).

6. **AUDIT-FIRST SEO & INDEXING:**
   - Check existing Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, Bing Webmaster, and Instant Indexing before modifying.
   - If properly connected, leave untouched. If missing or misconfigured, connect cleanly.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step 10-Phase Autonomous Workflow

### Phase 1: Site Audit & Credentials Inspection
- Inspect WP theme, active plugins, and database state.
- Check existing pages, categories, and published posts.

### Phase 2: Theme Layout & General Settings
- Set Site Width to `1400px`.
- Configure top trending bar (auto-scroll ON).
- Disable bottom latest posts bar and single post featured image overlay.

### Phase 3: Media Resizer & Single Post Polish
- Enable Media Resizer for 600x400 px centered images and 16:9 videos.
- Go to **Themixify -> Share Bar** and turn OFF "Share row under the title".

### Phase 4: Header & Primary Navigation
- Set clean SVG/WebP Site Logo and Favicon.
- Create/Assign Primary Menu with Home, Top 4-5 Categories, About Us, and Contact Us.
- Remove redundant duplicate search bars.

### Phase 5: Hero Banner & Visual Identity
- Upload 2-3 high-resolution, textless WebP banner slides.
- Write compelling headline, sub-headline, and high-contrast CTA buttons.

### Phase 6: Category Curation ("Browse by topic")
- Assign dedicated, textless WebP images for every category in `Posts -> Categories`.
- Never allow fallback to blog post thumbnails with printed titles.

### Phase 7: Legal Pages & EEAT Compliance
- Ensure standard pages exist: About Us, Contact Us, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, Disclaimer.
- Verify pages have clean editorial content without duplicate titles or dummy links.

### Phase 8: Leveled 3-Column Footer
- Configure Footer Widgets: Column 1 (About Brand), Column 2 (Categories), Column 3 (Legal Links).
- Level all columns symmetrically.

### Phase 9: Redirection & SEO Health
- Enable 404-to-homepage redirection in Themixify Redirect settings.
- Verify XML sitemap and indexing readiness.

### Phase 10: Final Quality Audit & Proof Screenshots
- Visit homepage, category archive, and single post.
- Capture full-viewport proof screenshots and present to Owner.
